# Task 08 v1: fact/inference competitive report

Using only the supplied evidence below, write a decision memo of at most 500 words.
Separate verified facts, inferences, unknowns, and recommended next checks. Every
fact must cite an evidence ID; inferences must name the facts they depend on. Do
not invent traffic, revenue, customer, pricing, or conversion data.

- E1: Competitor A's captured homepage shows online booking and three service tiers.
- E2: Competitor B's captured pricing page lists a $149 diagnostic visit.
- E3: Two public reviews mention slow callbacks; the sample is not representative.
- E4: Our approved brief prioritizes same-day scheduling and transparent estimates.

Return the memo only. Do not score it or mention model/provider identity.
