## Boundary

The website may reserve and preview a typed acquisition presentation slot. The appointment contract owns evidence, classifier output, exact public promise, target approval, signed publication, runtime downshift, recovery, and blocking. Website deployment and appointment activation retain separate identities and receipts.

The architecture is approved; implementation remains unauthorized until its acceptance and verification gates are satisfied.
