## Still missing from the intake

Not claims — gaps. A second intake pass should collect these, because
commercial buyers ask for them and their absence costs work:

1. **Insurance and COI readiness.** GCs will not let an uninsured sub on site.
2. **Applicable Florida low-voltage licensing.** "No certifications" answers a
   different question; licensing is not a certification.
3. **Warranty terms** — labour, and any manufacturer warranty WITS can extend.
4. **Testing and handover documentation** — what the customer actually receives.
5. **Occupied-building and after-hours working.** A live office fit-out is a
   different sale from a new build.
6. **Minimum job size and typical lead time.**
7. **Owner name and history.** "Personal service" is a stated differentiator,
   and there is currently no person anywhere on the page carrying it.
8. **One photographable reference project**, with customer permission.
9. **Real phone, email, domain, logo, brand colours.**
