## Selection rule applied

Per `DESIGN.md` §8, direction comes from Refero style `b11e1e78` (Ambrook):
*"candid, slightly desaturated photography … real people in natural or work
environments"*.

Deliberately **rejected** from the candidate set: every glowing-blue abstract
fibre-optic shot. That imagery is the dominant stock cliché for this trade and
contradicts the restraint of the design system — it signals "telecom brochure",
not "documented cable plant". Also rejected: a data-centre shot carrying a
visible third-party brand mark.

## Replacement brief

When WITS commissions its own photography:

> 16:9 and 4:3, colour. Candid and slightly desaturated. Real work in progress —
> a technician terminating a patch panel, a clean IDF with labelled runs, a rack
> mid-build. Natural light. No posed stock, no stage-lit server rooms, no
> imagery of another company's install. Shot on a WITS job site with the
> customer's written permission.

One photographable reference project is listed as an outstanding intake gap in
`CLAIMS.md`.
